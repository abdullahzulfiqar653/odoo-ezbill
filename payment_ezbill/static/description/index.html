EZBILL: Raast QR payments for Odoo

Get paid in Pakistan from any bank app, JazzCash or Easypaisa

EZBILL lets Pakistani businesses collect payments over Raast, Pakistan's instant payment system. At checkout your customer sees a dynamic QR code with the exact amount. They scan it with any bank app, JazzCash or Easypaisa, confirm, and Odoo marks the order paid automatically. No card details, no manual bank transfer checks.

  • Dynamic Raast QR on a clean payment page that updates by itself once paid
  • Signed webhooks (HMAC-SHA256) confirm the payment even if the customer closes the page
  • Sandbox (Test Mode) and live keys, with a safety check against mixing them up
  • Works with eCommerce, invoices and the customer portal (Odoo 17 and 18)
  • Amounts in PKR, settled to your bank account

Set up in minutes

  1. Install the module and open Payment Providers > EZBILL.
  2. Paste your API key and webhook secret from your EZBILL account.
  3. Copy the webhook URL into EZBILL, test in sandbox, then enable.
